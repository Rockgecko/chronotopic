The First Industrial Revolution marked a major turning point in human history. This period saw the transition from manual production methods to machine manufacturing, particularly in the textile industry. Key innovations included:

- The spinning jenny (1764)
- The water frame (1769)
- The steam engine improvements by James Watt (1769-1784)
- The power loom (1784)

This transformation led to:

1. Increased production efficiency
2. Urbanization
3. Rise of the factory system
4. Creation of a new working class
5. Significant environmental changes

The effects of this revolution were first felt in Britain before spreading to other parts of Europe and North America.
