The Great Pyramid of Giza was built as a tomb for the Pharaoh Khufu of the Fourth Dynasty of ancient Egypt. It is the oldest and largest of the three pyramids in the Giza pyramid complex. Originally standing 146.5 metres high, it was the tallest man-made structure in the world for more than 3,800 years.
