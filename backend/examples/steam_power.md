James Watt's separate condenser steam engine patent marked a crucial improvement in steam engine efficiency. This innovation allowed steam to be condensed in a separate chamber, maintaining the cylinder's heat and dramatically improving fuel efficiency.

The patent's monopoly rights lasted until 1800, significantly impacting the spread of steam power during the early Industrial Revolution.
